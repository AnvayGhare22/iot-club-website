ABOUT THE EVENT

What is IOT Hackathon?

The IOT Club Hackathon is a premier 24-hour coding marathon where innovation meets technology. This event brings together the brightest minds from across India to solve real-world problems using Internet of Things, Artificial Intelligence, and cutting-edge technologies.

Whether you're a seasoned developer or a curious beginner, this hackathon offers the perfect platform to learn, build, and showcase your skills. Work alongside industry mentors, access exclusive workshops, and compete for incredible prizes while making lasting connections.

HAVE QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate in the hackathon?

The hackathon is open to all college students across India, regardless of their branch or year of study. Both beginners and experienced developers are welcome!

What is the team size?

Teams can have 2-4 members. You can register as a team or join as an individual and we'll help you find teammates during the team formation session.

What should I bring to the hackathon?

Bring your laptop, chargers, student ID, and enthusiasm! We'll provide WiFi, food, workspace, and all the support you need to build something amazing.

Is there any registration fee?

No! The hackathon is completely free to participate. We'll also provide meals, snacks, and refreshments throughout the event.

Will there be mentors available?

Yes! Industry professionals and experienced developers will be available throughout the hackathon to guide you, answer questions, and help debug your code.
